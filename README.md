# Quantick Tech Website

This is the official website for Quantick Tech, built with a modern, high-performance tech stack.

## 🚀 Deployment

The site is optimized for **Vercel** and **GitHub Pages**.

### How to Activate the Contact Form

The contact form is powered by **FormSubmit.co**. To start receiving emails:

1.  **Submit the form** once on the live site.
2.  **Check your email**: You will receive an automated email from **FormSubmit** at **quanticktech@zohomail.in** asking you to confirm your email address.
3.  **Click "Activate Form"**: Once you click that button, the form is fully active and all future messages will go directly to your inbox.

No account setup is required!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5** & **Vanilla CSS**
- **JavaScript** (AJAX Form Handling, Reveal Animations)
- **Lucide Icons**
- **Vercel** (Clean URLs enabled)
